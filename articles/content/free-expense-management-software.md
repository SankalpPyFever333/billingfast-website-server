## Managing Business Expenses in 5 Steps

![Recording Expenses](/assets/images/expense-management/record-expense.png)

1. Navigate to Expenses > Record Expenses
2. Click "New Expense"
3. Choose a bank or cash account
4. Select Expense Date
5. Specify expense details and save
