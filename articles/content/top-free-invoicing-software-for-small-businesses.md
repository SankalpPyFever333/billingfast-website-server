## What Is An Invoice?

Invoices are official itemized receipts that break down transactions, listing:

- Products and services
- Quantities and prices
- Taxes and fees
- Shipping costs
- Payment terms

They serve as legal transaction records helping businesses track sales, manage
cash flow, and ensure tax compliance.
