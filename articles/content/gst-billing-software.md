## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the advantages of using BillingFast over Excel-based invoicing?

BillingFast automates GST calculations, maintains proper invoice sequences, and files returns directly - eliminating manual errors and saving hours of work.

### Can I amend invoices after creating them?

Yes, you can create credit notes and debit notes to amend invoices as per GST guidelines.

### Can I create export invoices?

Yes, BillingFast supports export invoices with proper GST compliance including LUT/Bond details.

### What GST voucher types are supported?

BillingFast supports all GST voucher types including Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply, Credit Note, Debit Note, and Delivery Challan.

### Does the software auto-generate invoice sequences?

Yes, invoice numbers are automatically generated in sequence as per GST requirements.

### Are GST rates and HSN/SAC codes pre-populated?

Yes, common GST rates and HSN/SAC codes are pre-populated for quick invoice creation.
