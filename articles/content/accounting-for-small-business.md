# How to Do Accounting for Small Business

**A complete guide to essential accounting practices for small business owners.**

![Accounting for Small Business](/assets/images/small-business-accounting/header.png)

## Introduction

This guide covers essential accounting practices for small business owners to maintain financial health and ensure efficient operations.

## Simple Bookkeeping for Small Business

### 1. Separate Personal and Professional Finances

Keep business and personal money separate using source documents. This is essential for:

- Accurate financial tracking
- Proper tax filing
- Clean audit trails

### 2. Maintaining Records

Track daily recurring transactions consistently. Good record-keeping:

- Aids in analyzing business financial situation
- Critical for filing tax returns without complications
- Helps identify trends and opportunities

### 3. Ledger

A ledger is an account summarizing transaction history. It appears on:

- Balance sheets
- Income statements

### 4. Trial Balance

Two types exist:

**Unadjusted Trial Balance**
Initial check of account balances after reporting period.

**Adjusted Trial Balance**
Balance prepared after adjusting entries, before financial statements.

### 5. Adjusting Entries

Made at period-end, addressing five common categories:

- Earned revenue
- Accrued expenses
- Unearned revenue
- Prepaid revenues
- Depreciation

### 6. Financial Reports

Four primary statements every business needs:

- Net profit statement
- Income statement
- Financial position statement (Balance Sheet)
- Stockholder equity statement

## Accounting System for Small Business

### 1. Bank Account

- Register a dedicated business account
- Track all incoming and outgoing transactions immediately
- Never mix personal and business finances

### 2. Accounting Method

Three options available:

**Cash Basis**
Most straightforward method. Tracks actual cash flow - income recorded when received, expenses when paid.

**Accrual Basis**
Records income and expenses regardless of payment timing. Shows a more accurate picture of financial health.

**Hybrid Method**
Combines cash and accrual approaches based on transaction type.

### 3. Track Transactions

- Monitor all business transactions systematically
- Use accounting software or hire professional support
- Keep receipts and documentation organized

### 4. Accounting Reports

- Compile financial data post-transaction monitoring
- Generate reports based on business progress
- Review regularly for insights

### 5. Payment Plan

- Establish consistent invoicing systems for credit transactions
- Maintain organized collection procedures
- Follow up on overdue payments promptly

## Accounting Tips for Small Businesses

### Keep Everything Organized

- Create a filing system for receipts
- Use digital tools to scan and store documents
- Back up your data regularly

### Reconcile Accounts Monthly

- Compare bank statements with your records
- Identify discrepancies immediately
- Catch errors before they compound

### Plan for Taxes

- Set aside money for tax payments throughout the year
- Track deductible expenses
- Keep tax-related documents accessible

### Review Financial Statements Regularly

- Monthly review of income and expenses
- Quarterly review of overall financial health
- Annual comprehensive analysis
