# Free Accounting Software for Small Businesses

**Compare the best free accounting software options for your small business.**

![Free Accounting Software](/assets/images/free-accounting-compare/header.png)

## Introduction

Maintaining different kinds of balance sheets and keeping track of all bank accounts while managing cash flow can be very time-consuming for small business owners. Free accounting software can help streamline these tasks.

In this guide, we compare three popular free accounting software options to help you make the right choice.

## Top 3 Free Accounting Software Compared

### 1. Wave Accounting

![Wave](/assets/images/free-accounting-compare/wave.png)

**Features:**

- Unlimited income/expense tracking
- Invoice creation
- Multi-business support
- Receipt scanning

**Limitations:**

- Cannot track accounts payable
- Lacks comprehensive mobile app
- Currently US-only availability

**Best For:** US-based small businesses needing basic accounting features.

---

### 2. GNUCash

![GNUCash](/assets/images/free-accounting-compare/gnucash.png)

**Type:** Free, open-source software

**Compatibility:** Linux, Windows, Mac-OS

**Features:**

- Double-entry accounting
- Stock/bond tracking
- Small business accounting

**Drawbacks:**

- Outdated interface
- Difficult installation process
- No customer support available

**Best For:** Tech-savvy users who want complete control and don't mind older interfaces.

---

### 3. BillingFast

![BillingFast](/assets/images/free-accounting-compare/billingfast.png)

**Type:** Online accounting software

**Highlights:**

- User-friendly interface
- Integrated bookkeeping services
- GST templates and compliance
- Inventory tracking
- Multi-currency support

**Pricing:**

- **Startup Plan:** Free with monthly invoice limits
- **Paid Plans:** Starting at Rs.499/month (India) or $15/month (international)

**Support:** Robust customer support team with prompt and responsive service

**Best For:** Small to mid-sized businesses needing comprehensive features with excellent support.
