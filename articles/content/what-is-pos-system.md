# What Is POS System and How It Works?

**A complete guide to understanding Point-of-Sale systems for your business.**

![POS System](/assets/images/pos/header.png)

## What Is a Point-of-Sale System?

A Point-of-Sale (POS) system is the platform handling purchase transactions. It represents the moment when a potential client finally transitions into a paying customer.

Modern POS systems have become increasingly digital, making them accessible to both business owners and customers. The system varies by business type and industry.

## How POS Systems Work

![How POS Works](/assets/images/pos/how-it-works.png)

A POS system follows these steps:

1. **Collects Orders**: Analog systems scan barcodes; digital systems collect data during shopping
2. **Records Items**: All items are added to customer's digital shopping cart
3. **Calculates Total**: System prepares final amount for payment
4. **Accepts Payment**: Processes payment and generates receipt (printed or digital)

## Types of POS Systems

### Traditional POS

- Fixed terminal at checkout counter
- Requires dedicated hardware
- Best for retail stores with fixed locations

### Mobile POS (mPOS)

- Uses smartphones or tablets
- Portable and flexible
- Ideal for small businesses and pop-up shops

### Cloud-Based POS

- Data stored in the cloud
- Accessible from anywhere
- Automatic updates
- Lower upfront costs

### Self-Service Kiosks

- Customer-operated terminals
- Reduces labor costs
- Faster service during peak hours
