# Best Inventory Management Software - Reviews

**A comprehensive comparison of the top inventory management solutions for

## Introduction

Inventory management can be a cumbersome task, especially if you have a large
inventory to take care of for your company. The right kind of inventory
management is the key to success for a startup as well as a well-established
company.

In this guide, we review the best inventory management software options
available to help you make an informed decision.

## Top 10 Inventory Management Software

### 1. BillingFast

![BillingFast](/assets/images/reviews/billingfast.png)

**The easiest & free inventory management software**

BillingFast is perfect for manufacturers and wholesalers selling online. It
offers a simple user interface with integrated accounting.

**Key Features:**

- Simple, intuitive user interface
- Integrated accounting with inventory
- Multiple warehouse support
- Sales orders and delivery documentation
- Stock control and adjustments
- Purchase management workflow
- Balance sheet and profit/loss reports

**Limitations:**

- No multi-channel order management

**Pricing:** Free for single user; affordable plans for additional users

---

### 2. NetSuite ERP's Inventory Management

**Enterprise-grade inventory solution**

**Key Features:**

- Real-time visibility across multiple locations
- Cloud-based integration platform
- Compatible with Salesforce and existing CRM systems
- End-to-end supply chain management

**Drawbacks:**

- Not compatible with Linux and Windows mobile systems
- Geared toward enterprises, not small businesses
- Support via ticketing system only (no live chat)

**Pricing:** Custom quote-based pricing by modules and users

---

### 3. Vend

**Point-of-sale inventory for retailers**

Designed primarily for retailers, runs on iPad.

**Strengths:**

- Strong inventory capabilities
- Central product catalog for variants
- Centralized reporting
- eCommerce and in-store functionality

**Limitations:**

- English language only
- Doesn't support Linux, Android, or Windows Mobile

**Pricing:** Lite ($99/month single outlet), Pro ($119/month multiple outlets)

---

### 4. Zoho Inventory

**Online inventory tracking across multiple channels**

Best for retail or trading businesses; small to medium-sized companies.

**Strengths:**

- Intuitive UI requiring no special accounting training
- Multi-channel integration (Amazon, eBay, Shopify)
- Partnerships with 25+ shipping carriers
- 24/7 customer support

**Limitations:**

- FIFO-only tracking method
- Requires separate integration with POS
- Basic reporting; no forecasting

**Pricing:** Free for <20 orders/month; $199-$249/month for paid plans

---

### 5. SAP Business One

**Enterprise financial and inventory solution**

**Strengths:**

- Extensive analytics and forecasting
- Supports four costing methods (FIFO, Moving Average, Standard Cost,
  Serial/Batch)
- Wide vertical application

**Limitations:**

- Not intuitive; requires documentation support
- Not suitable for small businesses

**Pricing:** One-time license ($3,213 professional; $1,666 limited)

---

### 6. Cin7

**All-in-one platform with POS, EDI, and logistics**

**Capabilities:**

- Integrates with 100+ third-party applications
- Multi-market inventory management
- Real-time visibility across multiple companies

**Drawbacks:**

- English only
- Dated interface; steeper learning curve

**Pricing:** $299/month minimum

---

### 7. Fishbowl Inventory

**Hybrid manufacturing and warehouse management**

For small and medium-sized businesses.

**Features:**

- Material requirement planning (MRP)
- Work order management
- Real-time shipment tracking
- Automatic reordering

**Weaknesses:**

- Steep learning curve
- Standard reporting only
- Unreliable customer support

**Pricing:** Starts at $4,395 per license

---

### 8. Brightpearl Inventory

**Sales and inventory accounting integration**

**Capabilities:**

- Real-time reporting
- Customer behavior analysis
- Handles up to 25,000 orders per hour

**Requirements:**

- Minimum $1M annual business turnover
- English language only

**Pricing:** Custom quotes (estimated $5,000+)

---

### 9. QuickBooks Inventory Management

**Advanced module within QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise**

**Capabilities:**

- Real-time tracking
- Barcode scanning
- Pick, pack, ship support
- Customizable reporting

**Limitations:**

- Scalability capped at 30 users
- Desktop-based; unsuitable for multi-location operations

**Pricing:** Silver ($1,039/year), Gold ($1,350), Platinum ($1,660)

---

### 10. EZ Office Inventory

**Asset lifecycle tracking specialist**

**Features:**

- Depreciation management
- Mobile app with RFID, barcode, and QR scanning
- Equipment distribution tracking
- Maintenance history logs

**Limitations:**

- No forecasting capability
- Designed for fixed asset management, not retail

**Pricing:** $270/month for unlimited users

---
