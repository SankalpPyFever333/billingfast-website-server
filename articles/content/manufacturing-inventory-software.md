## Track Inventory At Every Stage

**From consumption to production, track inventory during the manufacturing
process.**

### Purchase Tracking

Add additional costs such as freight and duty for each purchase order during the
purchase process. This means you know exactly what your products cost you, to
ensure you're setting a profitable sell price.

### Sales Tracking

Create sales orders and delivery challans when you ship the products. Later you
can create the invoice from a sales order in one click. Collect online payments
from customers & get reports on accounts receivables.

### Inventory Tracking

Record raw material consumption & production of finished goods. Easily record
stock wastage occurred during the manufacturing process & stock transfers
between warehouses.
